Environmental Pollution

Environmental pollution is the introduction of harmful substances into the environment which causes damage to air, water, land, and living organisms. Pollution affects human health and disturbs the balance of nature.

Why Save the Environment?

Saving the environment is important to ensure a healthy life for present and future generations. Small actions by individuals can bring big positive changes.
