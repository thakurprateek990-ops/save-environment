What is Environmental Pollution?

Environmental pollution refers to the introduction of harmful substances into the environment that cause damage to air, water, land, and living organisms. Pollution affects human health and disturbs the natural balance of the Earth.

Why Should We Protect the Environment?

Protecting the environment is essential for the survival of future generations. A clean and healthy environment ensures better living conditions for humans, animals, and plants.
