How Can We Protect the Environment?

Environmental protection starts with simple habits. The most effective methods are Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle.

Reduce
Reduce means using fewer resources and avoiding unnecessary waste. Saving water, electricity, and reducing plastic use helps prevent pollution.
Reuse
Reuse means using items again instead of throwing them away. Reusing bags, bottles, and containers reduces waste and saves resources.
Recycle
Recycle means converting waste materials into new useful products. Recycling helps reduce landfill waste and protects natural resources.
